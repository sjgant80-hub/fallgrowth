FallGrowth ◈

The growth brain of an AI-first company. It learns which social + ecommerce strategy moves you toward your goal — from real outcomes, not guesses. It picks the next move (the 90%); you approve anything that spends money or posts (your 10%); the real result teaches it.

The honest wire: this is a goal-weighted feedback ledger + bandit (witness-gated 46/49), not magic ML — Beta posteriors per lever, auditable arithmetic you can read. It learns only from real numbers you feed it: ecommerce outcomes from your CRM, engagement you paste weekly. No real data in = it's a scorer with extra steps. Everything is local to this browser (IndexedDB); nothing uploads.

1 · Your goal — what "winning" means

Weight the signals. The SAME post scores differently depending on what you're aiming at — that's how it learns for your goal, not a generic "best".

The starter seeds a real goal + 6 lever-hypotheses for an AI-first sovereign-tools company whose gap is recurring revenue. They're starting guesses — the organ learns which actually win once you log real outcomes.

2 · The levers (arms)

The named things you actually control. Seed a handful; the organ decides which to pull.

3 · The next move — the organ decides (your 90%)

It picks the lever-set to try next, and says why. Uncertain arms get explored; proven winners get exploited.

4 · Log the real outcome — this is the whole game

After a run, feed it the real numbers (normalise each 0–1: your best-ever ≈ 1). Ecommerce/revenue from your CRM; engagement pasted from the platform. The goal-weights turn these into one reward, and the chosen arm learns.

5 · The board — what it has learned

Every lever-set ranked by its posterior. Mean = how good it's proven so far; ceiling = optimistic best; pulls = real tests. The bar is the mean; the pick (◈) is what it'd run next.
